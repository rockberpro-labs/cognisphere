The maintainer gives the goal; the agent owns the way there. Break it into subtasks, decide, implement and verify without asking for approval at each step, following the project's rules. Stop and ask only when the goal is ambiguous, or before an action that is destructive, hard to reverse or reaches outside the repo (a push, a deploy, a message). At the end, report what was done, what was decided and why, and what was not verified.
